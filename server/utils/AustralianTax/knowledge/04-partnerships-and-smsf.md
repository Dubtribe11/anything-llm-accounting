# Partnerships and Self-Managed Super Funds

## Partnerships (Division 5 ITAA 1936)

A partnership for tax purposes is broader than the general law definition — it includes persons **in receipt of income jointly**, so co-owners of a rental property are a tax law partnership even without a business.

- The partnership lodges a return and obtains a TFN (and an ABN/GST registration if carrying on an enterprise), but it **pays no tax**.
- Each partner includes their **share of the partnership net income or loss** in their own return, at their own rate.
- A "partner's salary" is **not deductible** — it is simply a way of describing a profit share. The same applies to interest on partners' capital.
- Superannuation for partners is a personal deductible contribution, not a partnership deduction.
- Partners are **jointly and severally liable** for partnership debts — the main reason partnerships are avoided for trading businesses.
- A change in partners generally dissolves the old partnership and forms a new one, with CGT event(s) on the partnership assets. Subdiv 122-B and the small business restructure rollover can help.
- Losses flow to the partners, subject to **Division 35** non-commercial loss rules for individual partners.
- **PSI rules** override partnership allocations where income is for a partner's personal skills.

A common structure is a **partnership of discretionary trusts**, which combines the flexibility of partnership profit sharing with trust-level income splitting and asset protection.

## Self-Managed Superannuation Funds

### Tax rates

| Income | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| Concessional contributions and investment income (accumulation phase) | 15% |
| Capital gains on assets held **more than 12 months** | 15% with a 33⅓% discount = **10% effective** |
| Capital gains on assets held 12 months or less | 15% |
| Income supporting retirement phase pensions (ECPI) | **0%** |
| Non-arm's length income (NALI) and non-arm's length expenditure | **45%** |

Excess franking credits are **refundable** to a complying fund — a major reason for holding fully franked Australian shares in super.

### Exempt current pension income

ECPI is claimed by either:
- the **segregated method** — assets are specifically set aside to support retirement phase liabilities, or
- the **proportionate method** — an **actuarial certificate** determines the exempt proportion for the year.

A fund with disregarded small fund assets (a member with a total super balance over $1.6m who is in retirement phase in any fund) must use the proportionate method.

### Contributions

| | 2024-25 | 2025-26 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Superannuation guarantee rate | 11.5% | **12%** |
| Concessional cap | $30,000 | $30,000 |
| Non-concessional cap | $120,000 | $120,000 |
| Bring-forward (3 years) | $360,000 | $360,000 |
| Total super balance threshold for NCCs | $1.9m | **$2.0m** |
| General transfer balance cap | $1.9m | **$2.0m** |
| Division 293 threshold | $250,000 | $250,000 |

- **Carry-forward concessional contributions**: unused cap from the previous 5 years can be used if the total super balance at the prior 30 June was under $500,000.
- **Bring-forward non-concessional contributions**: available under 75, with the number of years available depending on the total super balance at the prior 30 June.
- **Personal deductible contributions** require a valid **notice of intent** (s 290-170) acknowledged by the fund before the return is lodged.
- **Division 293** adds 15% (total 30%) on concessional contributions where income plus contributions exceeds $250,000.
- Contributions count in the year the **fund receives** them.

### Compliance obligations

- **Sole purpose test** — the fund must be maintained solely to provide retirement benefits.
- **In-house assets** — investments in, loans to, or leases with related parties are limited to **5%** of fund assets. Business real property is an exception and can be 100%.
- **No loans or financial assistance to members or relatives** — ever.
- **Arm's length dealings** — breaches trigger NALI at 45%.
- **Investment strategy** — must be in writing, reviewed regularly, and address risk, return, diversification, liquidity and insurance for members.
- **Annual audit** by an approved SMSF auditor before lodging the SAR.
- **Limited recourse borrowing arrangements (LRBAs)** — permitted for a single acquirable asset held on a bare trust; safe harbour terms are set out in PCG 2016/5. LRBA balances count towards a member's total super balance in some circumstances.
- **Market valuation** of all assets at 30 June each year.

### Conditions of release

Preservation age is **60** for everyone born on or after 1 July 1964. Common conditions of release: retirement after preservation age, reaching 65, transition to retirement (restricted, non-commutable), death, permanent incapacity, terminal medical condition, severe financial hardship and compassionate grounds.

### Pending measure — Division 296

An additional 15% tax on earnings attributable to total super balances above $3 million has been announced and revised more than once. **Do not apply it without confirming the enacted legislation, threshold, indexation and start date.**

### Related-party property

A fund can acquire **business real property** and **listed securities** from a related party at market value. It cannot acquire residential property from a related party. Members and relatives must not use fund-owned residential property.
