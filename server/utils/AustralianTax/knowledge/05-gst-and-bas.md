# GST and Activity Statements

## The basics

GST is 10%. GST on a GST-inclusive price is **one eleventh** of that price.

Registration is required when **GST turnover** reaches:
- **$75,000** for a business,
- **$150,000** for a non-profit body,
- **$0** for taxi travel and ride-sourcing (registration required from the first dollar).

GST turnover is tested two ways — *current* turnover (this month plus the previous 11) and *projected* turnover (this month plus the next 11). Meeting either test triggers registration, which must occur within **21 days**.

## The three categories of supply

| Category | GST charged | Credits on related purchases | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Taxable** | Yes, 1/11 | Yes | Most goods and services |
| **GST-free** | No | **Yes** | Basic food, most health and medical, education courses, childcare, exports, sale of a going concern, farmland, water/sewerage, some precious metals |
| **Input taxed** | No | **No** | Residential rent, sale of existing residential premises, most financial supplies |

The distinction matters enormously: a GST-free supplier still claims all its input tax credits; an input taxed supplier does not.

## Input tax credits

A credit is available for the GST on a **creditable acquisition** — an acquisition for a creditable purpose, where the supply to you was taxable and you have provided (or are liable to provide) consideration.

- A **valid tax invoice** is required for purchases over **$82.50 (GST inclusive)**. It must show the supplier's identity and ABN, the date, a description, the GST amount (or a statement that the total includes GST), and for supplies of $1,000 or more, the recipient's identity or ABN.
- No credit for the **private use** portion, or for acquisitions relating to input taxed supplies.
- No credit for **entertainment** to the extent it is not deductible for income tax.
- Credits must be claimed within **four years** of the due date of the BAS for the period in which they arose.
- If a supplier has no ABN, **no ABN withholding** at 47% applies to the payment.

## Accounting basis

- **Cash basis** — available where GST turnover is under $10 million (or the entity is otherwise eligible). GST is attributed when payment is received or made.
- **Accruals (non-cash) basis** — GST is attributed on the earlier of an invoice being issued or any payment being made.

## Property and GST

- **New residential premises** — sale is taxable. Since 1 July 2018, the **purchaser** must withhold and remit GST directly to the ATO (1/11 of the price, or 7% under the margin scheme).
- **Existing residential premises** — input taxed. No GST on the sale, no credits on costs.
- **Commercial property** — taxable, unless sold as a **going concern** (GST-free where both parties are registered, they agree in writing, and everything necessary for the continued operation is supplied).
- **Margin scheme** — GST is 1/11 of the *margin* (sale price less the original purchase price or an approved valuation) rather than the full price. Requires written agreement before settlement and is not available where the property was acquired as a fully taxable supply without the margin scheme.

## Adjustments

- **Adjustment events** — cancellations, changes in consideration, changes in creditable purpose. Reported at 7 (increasing) and 4 (decreasing) or through 1A/1B depending on the form.
- **Bad debts** — a decreasing adjustment is available when a debt is written off as bad or is 12 months overdue (accruals basis).
- **Change in creditable purpose** — adjustment periods apply based on the GST-exclusive value of the acquisition.

## Other indirect taxes

- **Luxury car tax** — 33% on the GST-inclusive value above the threshold (separate, higher threshold for fuel-efficient vehicles).
- **Wine equalisation tax** — 29% of the wholesale value, with a producer rebate.
- **Fuel tax credits** — for fuel used in business, at different rates for heavy vehicles on public roads versus off-road use. Rates change each February and August.
